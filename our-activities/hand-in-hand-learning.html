Frequently Asked Questions

Who can volunteer?

Anyone can volunteer with Hand in Hand Learning. No teaching experience is needed.

How do I start?

First, register with Istanbul & I. Then join the HiH Learning group and complete the orientation.

How much time do I need?

You teach for about 1 hour each week. Our team plans the schedule, but lesson times can change when needed.

Do I need to teach for exactly one hour a week?

No, the minimum time per session is 30 minutes, and the maximum is 60 minutes (1 hour).

Do I have to volunteer every week?

No, you can volunteer whenever your schedule allows. You’re welcome to join whenever you’re available!

Can I observe a session before volunteering?

Yes! You can contact the officer responsible for that week’s session or the project manager to arrange to observe a session before getting started.

Can I volunteer as a guest?

Yes. Guests can teach too, but their teaching hours count toward the certificate only after they register with Istanbul & I.

Do I need to prepare my own lessons?

We provide the materials you need for your lessons. Feel free to use your own materials too.
